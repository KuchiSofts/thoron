## The logo

Compared by its own design rules (`branding/thoron/README.md`): Chrome's and Chromium's ring is cut by
straight lines that meet the inner circle; Thoron's three blades have curved edges and turn, in one violet
hue, around a white band and a violet core. Firefox (an animal around a globe), Edge (a wave), Brave (a lion),
Opera (an "O"), Vivaldi (a "V") and Thorium (its own atom-like mark) share no shape with it. Not compared by a
person outside the project yet.
