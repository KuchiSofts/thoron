# Native ad blocking: load times on the real network (Thoron 158.0.8127.3010)

Generated by `tools/qa/adblock_breakage.py time` on 186 pages: the home pages of the 99 sites of Tranco
list Y895G (`tools/qa/sites/tranco-Y895G-top99.txt`) and, for the 87 of them that have one, a page beyond
the home page (`adblock_breakage.py inner`: the most article-like link of the home page that stays on the
site). The list of those inner pages is not published: a site may send the browser to a page for the PC's
own country or language.

uBlock Origin alone and the native engine alone, each in its own throwaway profile, both open at once and
taking turns page by page (which goes first changes every page and every round), so a network that gets
slower or faster meets both alike. The time is from the start of the navigation to the end of the load
event; round 1 starts with empty caches. A ratio below 1 means the native engine was faster. Pages where
one of them did not load in 30 s are left out of that round.

| 1 | 184 | 1164.0 | 1154.0 | 0.950 | 113 of 184 |
| 2 | 184 | 947.5 | 950.0 | 0.947 | 114 of 184 |

On the real network the native engine loads a page about 5 % faster than uBlock Origin (the median of the
per-page ratios, both rounds), and faster on 61 % of the pages: the same result as the offline proof
(`2026-11-11-native-adblock-proof-turns.md`, 6.1 %). The medians of the two columns are close because a
real page's load time is mostly the network's; the per-page ratio takes that out.
